# streets_places

Street names, geometry corrections and key places for Fotouni, mostly produced by the street-editor maps and the community.

- `street_edits_20260915.csv` – street id -> name/rank/drivable edits.
- `edge_edits_20260915.csv` – per OSM edge (u, v, key) street assignment, geometry edits and new edges.
- `Fotouni_Sept. 2026.kml` – places (squares, facilities) drawn in Google Earth.
- `Overview Abbreviations.xlsx` – street-name abbreviations.
- `Squares- Intersections - Facilities.xlsx` – list of squares, intersections and facilities.

Generated by `code/fotouni_town_qc`:

- `bandja_streets_review.gpkg` (+ `.md`) - layers `edges`, `survey_gaps`, `places`, `street_name_check`, to revise in QGIS.
- `fotouni_area_candidates.gpkg` (+ `.md`) - candidate Fotouni extents (`area_candidates`) and the unnamed roads inside them (`unnamed_roads_in_fotouni`).
