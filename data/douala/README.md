# data/douala

- `pilot_survey/` – pilot building survey of Bonamoussadi (RTK GNSS + phone, SW Maps). `raw/` exports, built `douala.csv`, `streets.gpkg`, `survey_map.html`.
- `iuc/` – material from the Institut Universitaire de la Cote: `workshops/` (campus rasters and spreadsheets) and `dountsop/` (building footprints shapefile, QGIS project).
